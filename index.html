The two 22-game plans split the season. On both teams, Plan A and Plan B divide every dated home game between them with no overlap — 21 dates each on the Celtics side, 22 each on the Bruins side. The 10-game plans draw from both.

Row A vs Rows B. Both pricing sheets list two tiers per game — Row A (front of the balcony) runs $10 more than every other row. Plan pricing is a single number, so which tier you actually get changes the discount a lot.

Bergeron Night. Patrice Bergeron's No. 37 goes up before the Dec 1 Bruins game vs Colorado — the most expensive date on either sheet at $326.50. It appears in the 22-Game Bergeron package only. The sale email says the 22-Game Plan guarantees access to the ceremony, but the 22-Game Black Friday game list doesn't include Dec 1. Worth pinning down with the rep before you buy.

Celtics plan costs. The four Celtics package sheets list every game but carry no price. Until the sale email numbers land, those plans show Row A face value instead of a plan cost, and the cost columns in the table read “—”.

The pricing sheet has four wrong opponents. On four Celtics dates the pricing calendar disagrees with the package sheets: Oct 14, Dec 2 and Jan 23 are printed as Brooklyn but are Charlotte, and Mar 16 is printed as the Lakers but is Sacramento. The package sheets are right, and the published NBA schedule confirms all four. The pricing calendar also convicts itself twice over — it lists five home games against Brooklyn, where a division rival is two, and it lists the Lakers twice, as both LAL and LAK. This page uses the package sheets' opponents; the prices are unaffected, since only the labels differ.

NBA Cup. Dec 4–11 is a single window on the Celtics sheet with eight potential home dates, flat $71.50 / $61.50 and no opponent or tip time. Both 22-game plans include exactly one Cup game in it — Plan A a semifinal, Plan B a quarterfinal — which is why each is 21 dated games plus one Cup slot. That slot is counted in the face-value totals at Cup price; selecting either plan lights the whole window, since the actual date isn't known yet. The eight rows are otherwise excluded from every total.

Preseason. Bruins Sep 20 vs Washington and Sep 22 vs Philadelphia are preseason at $61.50 — each 22-game plan spends one of its 22 slots on one. The Celtics plans do the same: Oct 10 vs Philadelphia (Plan B) and Oct 14 vs Charlotte (Plan A), both marked preseason on the package sheets and both $61.50.

Double headers. Three dates put both teams in the building: Oct 10, Nov 27 and Dec 20. Dec 5 would be a fourth if the Celtics host an NBA Cup game that day. They're tagged DH in the combined view.
